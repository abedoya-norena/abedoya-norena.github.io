Earl The Giraff

Giraffes are tall mammals that live in parts of Africa. They are known for their long necks and spotted patterns. One thing I learned is that a giraffe's height helps it reach leaves that other animals cannot. Their tongues are also really long, which is kind of crazy. This page is part of my mini animal encyclopedia. Use the menu above to switch to the other animals.

Fun facts

  • The heaviest Giraff in the world weighed 400 pounds.
  • Giraff are the talles of all land animals in the world.
  • A newborn Giraff can stand up shortly after being born.

Where they live

Giraffes live in savannas and open woodlands. They usually spend a lot of time around trees like acacias.
