Tony The Tiger

Tigers are large carnivorous cats that live mainly in Asia. They are known for their orange fur and black stripes. Each tiger has a unique stripe pattern, similar to human fingerprints. Tigers are also very strong swimmers. This page is part of my animal encyclopedia for class. Use the navigation bar to explore the other animals.

Fun facts

  • The heaviest elephant in the world weighed 26,000 pounds.
  • Elephants are the largest of all land animals in the world.
  • A newborn elephant can stand up shortly after being born.

Where they live

Tigers live in forests, grasslands, and mangrove swamps. They need large territories to hunt and survive.
